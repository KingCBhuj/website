A place for life.

What would social media look like if it helped us live well, rather than simply spend more time online? That is the question behind Utopia.

Our proposed direction: thoughtful conversations, a chronological feed, practical experiments, and respectful disagreement. These are starting ideas—not a fixed worldview.

Everyday life

Relationships, health, meaningful work, and the things that make life worthwhile.

Community & nature

Local cooperation, gardening, self-reliance, and caring for the places we live.

Ideas & questions

Simple living, freedom, technology, culture, and different visions of a good life.

Start with one small experiment.

Examples of the kinds of experiences members could share:

  • Trying a month without short-video apps.
  • Growing food on a balcony or terrace.
  • Buying from local makers and repairing instead of replacing.
  • Making more time for walking, friends, or family.

What did you try? What changed? What did not work?

First conversations

  • What does a good life mean to you?
  • What has modern life made unnecessarily complicated?
  • How could technology support life without dominating it?
  • What could we do better together than alone?
